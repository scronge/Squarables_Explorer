n7-c1

This page places the main geometric objects for one configuration side by side: the tiling itself, the corner graph, and the vertical and horizontal directed transversal graphs.

Tiling

The normalized square dissection itself.

Corner graph and planar dual

Undirected embedded plane graph: tile corners are vertices and maximal boundary segments are edges. The planar dual places one vertex in each tile and one edge across each shared positive-length boundary segment.

Planar dual

Vertical directed transversal

Directed graph built from horizontal boundary segments, with arrows running from the top pole to the bottom pole.

Superimposed view

Horizontal directed transversal

Directed graph built from vertical boundary segments, with arrows running from the left pole to the right pole.

Superimposed view

Horizontal bands

Horizontal band overlay showing the row-by-row compositions and their partition versions on the tiling itself.

Vertical bands

Vertical band overlay showing the column-by-column compositions and their partition versions on the tiling itself.
